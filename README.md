### ✨ Key Features

* 🎫 Online ticket booking
* 💺 Interactive seat selection
* 📅 Trip & schedule management
* 🚌 Transport company management
* 👤 Authentication & role-based access
* 📊 Administrative dashboards
* 📍 Bus tracking
* 📱 Responsive passenger experience

### 🛠️ Technology
